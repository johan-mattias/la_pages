New exploratory VaSSTra analysis · units 1–10

SCT-oriented activity trajectories

This explorer describes how students work, using two transparent composites—mastery versus withdrawal and distributed work—plus idle time in lessons, hard-exercise attempts, and pace relative to the class. The SCT survey is used for validation, not as an input to the trajectories.

This is an exploratory analysis. The five inputs are standardized within unit × class. States are fitted to active student-units; a zero state means no activity in that unit.
