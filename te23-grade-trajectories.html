Grade trajectories

How students moved from the February 2025 estimate through the unit 10 grade and the June 2025 estimate to their final grade (TE23), and how the graded tasks of the year led to the course grade in TE22 and te24c.

te23b n = 33, te23c n = 29; students who declined StudyBee use are left out (te23b: 2, te23c: 2, te22a: 3, te22b: 3, te22c: 4). te23b's final is the teacher's course grade, te23c's final is the final project grade. Counts only; no names or ids.

Do the trajectories make sense?

  • The estimates are steady. The February and June estimates agree closely: ρ 0.86 in te23b and 0.75 in te23c. Most students who move go up: from February to the final grade, 29 improved, 23 stayed and 9 went down.
  • Unit 10 is the noisy step. It is a single assignment, not an overall estimate. Several students drop to F on unit 10 and are back at C or B in the June estimate: 3 of the 4 unit 10 F's in te23b, 2 of the 3 unit 10 F's in te23c. That pattern looks like a missing or late submission rather than a real dip.
  • te23b's final grade follows the June estimate (ρ 0.87; nobody moves more than two steps). te23c's final moves much more (ρ 0.61; 8 down, 11 up, some by three or four steps). That's expected, because te23c's final is the project grade, one assessment, while te23b's is the teacher's overall grade. In both classes, every drop of two or more steps ends in F.
  • Gaps: te23c has one missing February estimate and one missing unit 10 grade, shown as "–".

TE22 and te24c: from the year's graded tasks to the course grade

TE22's grade files record the graded tasks and the course grade, not running estimates, so these diagrams follow task grades: te22a n = 24, te22b n = 29, te22c n = 28. te24c (n = 31) has Creative task 3, an estimate just before the final grade, and the final grade; its survey did not ask about StudyBee, so no te24c student is left out. "–" = no grade recorded for the task.

  • The course grade follows the final project, as in TE23: final project → course grade ρ 0.98 in te22b, 0.93 in te22c and 0.62 in te22a.
  • Early tasks predict less. The unit 4 task grade correlates 0.61 (te22b) and 0.31 (te22c) with the course grade. Most students finish at or above their unit 4 grade: 9 higher, 15 the same and 3 lower in te22b; 14, 6 and 4 in te22c.
  • te22a's first stage is pass/fail. Its unit 6 project is graded only E or F, so the step from there to the course grade is not a real rise; read te22a from the unit 7 task on.
  • Missing tasks are common in TE22, for example 10 of te22c's students have no unit 10 grade. Missing work goes with low course grades (Start & finish).
  • In te24c nobody went down. From Creative task 3 to the final grade, 15 students rose and 16 stayed; the estimate before the final predicts it closely (ρ 0.88).
