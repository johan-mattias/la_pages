Activity across the units · The story

Compare precomputed feature combinations, state solutions and trajectories. These are exploratory behavioural profiles, not validated self-efficacy levels.

Active units are median-imputed and standardized within unit × class. States are ordered by the equally weighted mean of their standardized features without reversing any feature. A higher-numbered state is not necessarily more favourable: individual features can move in opposite directions. Inactive is a separate sequence state.

Top five: outcome separation

Ranked by the equally weighted mean adjusted R² for self-efficacy, grade and goals / interest across trajectory groups. Each adjusted R² compares between-group differences with overall variation and penalizes additional groups; negative values are possible. Each outcome uses its available observations. A solution is excluded if any trajectory has fewer than five observed values for any outcome or an outcome has no variation.

These are in-sample, exploratory associations after comparing many solutions—not evidence of prediction, causation or validation. Group separation does not imply a consistent ordering across outcomes.
